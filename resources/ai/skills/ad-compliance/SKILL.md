# Ad compliance (visual)

Clients boost almost every post as a paid ad, so images must pass Meta's ad review and stay within Mongolian advertising rules. You make images, not claims — keep the picture honest and neutral. When in doubt choose the conservative version and note it in `finish`.

## Never make
- Tobacco, vapes, e-cigarettes, weapons, drugs or drug paraphernalia as the subject.
- Real, recognisable people (celebrities, politicians) or look-alikes; other companies' logos, packaging or trademarks.
- Mongolian state symbols, the Soyombo, banknotes or official seals (see `mongolian-culture`).
- Sexualised imagery; graphic injury, blood, medical procedures.
- Fake UI that looks clickable (play buttons, "Like" buttons, system notifications, checkboxes).

## Handle with care
- **Alcohol** — the product only (bottle, glass, bar ambience); no one drinking, no young-looking people, no driving, sport or work context, no implied effects (party success, confidence). Alcohol ads are age-restricted on Meta and restricted in Mongolia — keep it calm.
- **Health, supplements, medicines** — no before/after, no bodies "fixed", no doctors in white coats endorsing, no pills promising results; show the product and a healthy, neutral lifestyle.
- **Weight loss / fitness** — no before/after, no zoomed-in body parts, no measuring tapes around waists, no shaming. Show activity and energy, not body shape.
- **Cosmetics & beauty** — show the product and a natural, healthy look; no "perfect skin" transformations, no exaggerated results.
- **Finance, loans, crypto** — no piles of cash, no "get rich" luxury fantasies; show service, trust, people, the office or app context in a calm way.
- **Children** — fully clothed, safe, age-appropriate situations, with a parent where relevant; never in ads for adult products.
- **Food** — the dish should look like what the venue really serves (use their photo as reference).

## Claims
Images must not imply claims the client did not give (medical effects, "No. 1", awards, certifications). If the client asks for words with a claim on the image, keep exactly their words, nothing stronger.

## If the brief itself is not allowed
Make the closest acceptable version (e.g. a calm product shot instead of a before/after) and explain the choice in `finish` so the studio can tell the client.
