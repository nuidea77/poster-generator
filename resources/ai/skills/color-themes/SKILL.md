# Colour themes

Pick one theme per job and use it consistently across every image and clip. In prompts, describe the palette in words plus the dominant colours ("deep navy and teal palette with cream highlights"); models follow named colours better than hex codes. If the client has a brand colour, choose the theme whose mood matches and swap its primary for the brand colour.

## Custom theme

When none fits, build one the same way: a dark anchor, a primary, a secondary accent, a light neutral; name it after its mood. Check it against Mongolian colour meanings in `mongolian-culture` (e.g. avoid black-dominated designs for celebrations).
