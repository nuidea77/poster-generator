# People & lifestyle

## Casting
- Default to people who look like the client's customers: **Mongolian / East Asian** adults of the right age group, natural features, varied body types — unless the brief says otherwise.
- Describe them concretely: age range, hair, clothing, expression ("a Mongolian woman in her late 20s, shoulder-length black hair, cream knit sweater, relaxed smile").
- Dress for the season in Mongolia (see `mongolian-culture`): winter coats and hats Nov–Mar, light layers in spring/autumn, summer clothes Jun–Aug.
- Never a recognisable real person or look-alike; no children unless the brief is about them (then see `ad-compliance`).

## Poses that work with AI
- **One or two people** per image. Crowds produce faces and hands that break.
- People **doing something with the product**: sipping, holding at chest height from below, applying, wearing, walking in, laughing at a table. Avoid empty "posing to camera".
- Hands: holding objects from below or at the side, not intricate finger work (typing, writing, tying). If hands keep failing, crop them out or put the product on the table in front of the person.
- Faces: 3/4 view or profile is safer than straight-on close-ups; a soft-focus person behind a sharp product hides small flaws.

## Framing
- The product stays the hero: person in the background or at the side, product sharp in the foreground — unless the service itself is the subject (salon, gym, class).
- Crop at the waist, chest or mid-thigh — not at joints (wrists, knees, ankles).
- Keep faces inside the safe area of the ratio.

## Light & mood
Natural window light and warm interiors read as genuine; avoid over-retouched "stock photo" smiles. Name the setting precisely (a Ulaanbaatar apartment kitchen, a café by a window with snow outside, a gym with morning light).

## People in video
- One simple action per clip (turns, smiles, takes a sip, walks toward camera). No talking — models cannot lip-sync to a script.
- Prefer `veo` for realistic people; animate from an approved still so the person stays the same across clips.
- Keep the same person, outfit and setting described identically in every clip prompt.
