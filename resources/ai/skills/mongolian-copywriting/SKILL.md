# Mongolian marketing copy

## Tone
- Direct and warm. Use "та" (polite you) for services, finance, health; "чи"-free. Imperatives in -аарай/-ээрэй/-оорой/-өөрэй are friendly CTAs: "Ирээрэй", "Захиалаарай", "Бүртгүүлээрэй".
- Avoid literal translations of English slogans; prefer short native phrases: "Алдахгүй шүү!", "Зөвхөн энэ долоо хоногт", "Хязгаартай тоотой".
- Keep Latin only for brand names, product models and hashtags.

## Headline patterns (≤ 7 words)
- Offer + number: "Бүх кофе -30%"
- Event: "Их нээлт — 10.20"
- Benefit: "Өглөөг амттай эхлүүл"
- Question: "Шинэ зун, шинэ дүр төрх?"
- Announcement: "Шинэ цэс ирлээ"

## Numbers, dates, prices
- Dates: "10-р сарын 20" or short "10.20". Weekday names: Даваа, Мягмар, Лхагва, Пүрэв, Баасан, Бямба, Ням.
- Prices: "25,000₮" (thousands separator, ₮ after). Discounts: "-30%" or "30% хямдрал".
- Phone: "9911-2233". Time: "10:00–22:00".

## Captions
2–4 short lines: hook line → offer/details → CTA with location or link → hashtags on a separate line. Emojis sparingly (1–3), never in the headline.

## Hashtags
Mix: 3–4 broad Mongolian/English (#улаанбаатар #mongolia #ub), 3–4 category (#кофе #coffeeshop #хямдрал), 1–2 brand. 5–10 total, no spaces, Cyrillic is fine.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- "Бид таныг урьж байна" style bureaucratic phrasing — say "Ирээрэй" instead.
- Over-long headlines with commas; split into headline + subheadline.
- Mixed scripts inside one word; wrong vowel harmony in suffixes (хямдрал-тай, нээлт-ийн, кофе-ны).
