# Visual concept

Generic prompts give generic stock images. Before prompting, define a small aesthetic worldview for this job and express it consistently.

## 1. Name the concept (1–3 words)

Examples: "Warm Minimalism", "Steppe Light", "Chromatic Calm", "Neon Night Market", "Analog Morning", "Monumental Product".

## 2. Define it in four short rules

- **Form & space:** e.g. one monumental object with vast negative space / dense organic arrangement / strict grid of repeated items.
- **Colour & material:** e.g. two-colour palette with one hot accent; matte ceramics and linen; glossy acrylic and neon.
- **Light:** e.g. low golden side light with long shadows; soft overcast studio; hard noon sun with crisp shadows; neon rim light in darkness.
- **Composition:** e.g. subject on the lower third, sky above; symmetrical frontal; extreme close-up crop; overhead flat lay.

Write these rules into every prompt of the job so posters and clips feel like one series.

## 3. The subtle reference

Find a quiet conceptual thread from the brief and weave it into form and colour without announcing it — a café's morning ritual expressed as long morning light and steam; a gym's strength as heavy geometric forms; a bakery's warmth as golden crust tones in the whole palette. People who know the business feel it; everyone else simply sees a beautiful image.

## 4. Craft

The result should look like it was made by a top art director and photographer who laboured over every detail: deliberate light, clean edges, considered colour, nothing accidental. When refining, do not add more objects or effects — ask how to make what is already there more coherent and more striking. Fewer, stronger elements beat busy scenes.

## Examples

- **"Monumental Product"** — the product enormous and centred like a sculpture, low camera angle, pure colour-field background in the brand colour, one hard light from above, deep shadow.
- **"Analog Morning"** — film-grain photography, soft window light, linen and wood, muted warm palette, quiet everyday moment around the product.
- **"Chromatic Calm"** — colour blocks as the composition: the product sits where two brand-colour fields meet, flat even light, generous space.
