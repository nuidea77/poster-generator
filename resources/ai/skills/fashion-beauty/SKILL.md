# Fashion & beauty

## Keep the real item
Clothes, shoes, bags and cosmetics must stay identical to the client's photos — cut, colour, print, logo placement, bottle shape. Use `gemini` + references and describe only the new scene. Check seams, buttons, prints and label text on the result.

## Fashion shots
- **On model** — the garment as the subject: full-length for outfits, waist-up for tops, close-ups for details. A Mongolian / East Asian model suited to the target customer (see `people-lifestyle`), natural pose, walking or turning for movement.
- **Flat lay** — overhead, the outfit arranged with accessories on a clean textured surface; good for sets and new arrivals.
- **Ghost / hanging** — garment on a hanger or invisible mannequin against a plain wall; good for e-commerce feel.
- Light that shows fabric: soft side light for knitwear and texture, hard sun for summer looks, studio soft box for clean catalogue shots.
- Season: winter collections in snow / warm interiors, summer in Ulaanbaatar streets or steppe light.

## Beauty products
- Hero bottle on a surface that matches the ingredient story (water ripples, botanicals, stone, silk), with soft gradient light.
- **Textures**: cream swirl, serum drop, lipstick bullet, powder burst, swatches on a clean surface.
- Skin: real texture and pores, soft light, no plastic over-smoothing; no before/after results (see `ad-compliance`).

## Salons & services
Show the experience: a calm treatment room, hands at work at the edge of frame, the finished hair / nails / brows in a close-up, the interior with warm light. Keep faces partly turned or in soft focus to avoid artefacts.

## Jewellery
Macro close-ups, dark velvet or stone, small hard light for sparkle, or worn on skin (neck, hand) in soft light. Keep the design exact.

## Beauty & fashion reels
Fabric moving in wind, a slow turn of the model, a hand applying cream, a dropper releasing serum, a close-up rack focus from product to face. 5–8 s clips; end on the product with the logo still.
